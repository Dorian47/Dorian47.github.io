I am a third-year Ph.D. student in Information Engineering at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, advised by Prof. Soung Chang Liew in the Advanced Wireless Systems Group. Before this, I received my B.Eng. in Communication Engineering from Sun Yat-sen University in 2024.

I am a systems researcher working at the intersection of AI, wireless communication, and embedded systems. I am interested in building intelligent systems that operate effectively under real-world constraints. My previous work has focused on multimodal LLMs for semantic video streaming, real-time communication and coordination for robot control, and the design and implementation of heterogeneous LiFi–WiFi systems. Across these projects, I have worked on the full system stack—from algorithm and protocol design to SDR-based implementation and experiments with physical robots.

More recently, my research has shifted toward vision–language–action (VLA) model acceleration for embodied AI. I am particularly interested in the systems challenges behind deploying these models on real robotic platforms, including inference efficiency, communication latency, resource constraints, and the interaction between computation and physical control.

My broader goal is to understand how advances in AI can be translated into systems that are efficient, responsive, and reliable outside controlled environments.

Collaboration: I am open to research discussions and collaborations. Feel free to email me at xs024@ie.cuhk.edu.hk.

Research Highlights

SemABR: Multimodal models guide adaptive video streaming through semantic fidelity.

paper

We use multimodal models to check which objects, actions, and events are preserved at different video bitrates. SemABR runs this analysis offline, then uses the scores to choose bitrates during streaming over a 5G edge network.

The heatmap compares our semantic fidelity score (SF) with standard video metrics and human ratings (MOS). On LIVE-NFLX-II, SF has a Spearman correlation of 0.81 with MOS.

LightGuard: A spatially constrained LiFi link bootstraps secure WiFi sessions.

papercode
Demo video

LightGuard establishes session keys over a physically confined LiFi channel while retaining commodity WiFi for high-throughput data. The prototype combines commercial WiFi NICs with portable LiFi antennas and a custom RF front end.

The implementation is transparent to ordinary applications: the security bootstrap happens below the application layer.

Hybrid TDMA/CSMA: Deadline-aware wireless communication supports real-time robot control.

preprintcode
Demo video

This Openwifi-based MAC combines protected TDMA slots for deadline-critical commands with CSMA access for flexible background traffic. It is evaluated from SDR packet delivery all the way to robot motion.

The latest system includes a real-time SDR testbed, ROS–Gazebo path tracking, and closed-loop formation control on physical TurtleBot 2 robots over a live wireless channel.

CL-SEC: Language models and SDR enable real-time semantic error correction.

papercode

CL-SEC combines physical-layer soft information from an SDR receiver with language-model context to recover the intended text in real time, while preserving verbatim output rather than merely generating a plausible paraphrase.

The end-to-end prototype connects a conventional sender to a software-defined receiver and performs cross-layer correction on live wireless packets.
